# Kong AI Gateway Demo — Prep Recommendations
### ApexTrans Logistics · Technical CSM Interview · Adnan Bukhari

Recommendations in order of impact.

---

## 1. Reduce Demo Risk

**Check plugin access in the trial account today.**
Several features in the demo — AI Rate Limiting Advanced, AI MCP Proxy, and the semantic plugins — are enterprise AI features. Confirm the Konnect trial enables them before building the demo around them. If one isn't available, reply-all and ask; the email says there's no penalty for questions.

**Plan for the shared OpenAI key failing.**
Other candidates are probably using the same key, so it could hit rate limits or be revoked. Test it early, and record a full backup video of the demo while everything works.

**Don't type live.**
Put every curl command in a script or saved Insomnia requests, and use large fonts and a clean terminal. Configure plugins through the UI, where the panel can follow along.

**Rehearse the switches between slides and Konnect.**
Transitions are where the most time gets lost.

---

## 2. Present as a Technical CSM, Not Only a Solutions Engineer

The role is CSM, so the panel will look for customer-success thinking, not just product features. Add a short "how I'd run this account" moment:

- **Enablement:** Kong Academy paths for the 20 squads and an AI champion in each squad.
- **Regular touchpoints:** office hours during rollout and a review at each phase gate against the agreed KPIs.
- **Adoption health signals:** share of AI traffic through the gateway, squads onboarded, and incidents blocked.

This can go into the notes of the next-steps slide or become its own slide.

---

## 3. Use the Room

- Open by asking the panel one discovery question — for example: *"Before I start, what does success look like for you in the first 90 days?"* Then refer back to their answer throughout.
- Address each role by name at the points written into the speaker notes — for example: *"For our developer, here's what changes in your code."*
- Prepare two or three questions for the Q&A. Good ones: how Kong measures CSM success, and what the customer mix looks like for AI Gateway.

---

## 4. Handle What You Don't Know

If you get a question you can't answer, *"I'd confirm that in the docs and follow up"* beats guessing. The brief says breadth and understanding matter more than being an expert.

---

## 5. Finish the Logistics

- Port the deck into Kong's Google Slides template — the email asks for it.
- Fill in the `[__]` placeholders.
- Do one timed dry run end to end, targeting 28 minutes.
- Turn off notifications and close other tabs before screen sharing.
- After the interview, send a short thank-you with a link to the deck.

---

## 6. Keep Claims Factual

Avoid statistics you can't source. The deck deliberately uses no invented numbers, and Kong's panel will know the real ones.
